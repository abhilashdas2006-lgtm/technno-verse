🔐 Privacy Policy

How ResQLink handles and protects information provided through the platform.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

ResQLink is a disaster relief coordination platform designed to connect affected citizens, volunteers, NGOs, relief organizations and emergency responders.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when using the platform.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, ResQLink may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Emergency help requests
  • Approximate location information
  • Volunteer skills and availability
  • Organization information
  • Information submitted through forms

3. How Information Is Used

Information submitted to ResQLink may be used to coordinate disaster response and connect people with appropriate assistance.

  • Process emergency assistance requests
  • Coordinate volunteers and organizations
  • Identify areas requiring assistance
  • Coordinate relief supplies
  • Send important emergency notifications
  • Improve the platform and response process

4. Location Information

Location information can be important during disaster response because responders need to know where help is required.

📍 Location information should only be shared when necessary for the requested disaster-relief service or when the user has provided permission.

5. Emergency Information

Emergency requests may contain sensitive information about a person's situation. Such information should only be accessed by authorized response personnel and organizations when required for coordination.

6. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with authorized emergency responders, NGOs, volunteers or government authorities when necessary to coordinate disaster assistance.

ResQLink is designed to minimize unnecessary sharing of personal information.

7. Data Security

ResQLink aims to use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration or misuse.

However, no online system can guarantee complete security of information.

8. User Responsibility

Users should avoid submitting unnecessary sensitive information through public forms or messages.

Only information relevant to the emergency or coordination request should be provided.

9. Third-Party Services

ResQLink may use external services such as mapping, hosting or communication services to provide certain features.

Such services may have their own privacy policies and terms of use.

10. Cookies and Local Storage

The demonstration version of ResQLink may use browser storage or similar technologies to remember settings and preferences.

11. Children's Privacy

ResQLink is intended to be used under appropriate supervision during disaster-response activities. Users should not submit personal information about children unless necessary and authorized.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the ResQLink platform develops. Changes will be reflected on this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the ResQLink support team.
