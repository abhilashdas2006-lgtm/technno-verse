About ResQLink

ResQLink is a disaster relief coordination platform designed to connect affected citizens, volunteers, NGOs, emergency responders and authorities through one centralized system.

🚨 The Problem

During disasters, thousands of people may need help at the same time. Information can become scattered between phone calls, social media, messages and different organizations. This makes it difficult to identify who needs help, where help is required and which resources are available.

📍

Lack of Location Information

Emergency teams may struggle to quickly identify the exact locations where help is required.

📦

Resource Mismatch

Relief supplies may exist in one location while another affected area urgently needs those resources.

📢

Communication Gaps

Citizens, NGOs, volunteers and authorities may not have one centralized platform for coordination.

💡 Our Solution

ResQLink brings disaster response activities into one centralized digital platform. Citizens can request assistance while responders can monitor requests, resources and emergency situations in real time.

  • ✓ Centralized emergency requests
  • ✓ Live disaster mapping
  • ✓ Volunteer coordination
  • ✓ NGO and relief organization coordination
  • ✓ Relief supply tracking
  • ✓ Emergency alerts

One Platform. One Response.

Connect the right people, resources and information at the right place when every second matters.

🔄 How ResQLink Works

The platform creates a simple flow between affected citizens and disaster responders.

1

Report

Citizens submit a request for emergency assistance.

2

Verify

Response teams review and prioritize the request.

3

Coordinate

NGOs and volunteers are assigned to the situation.

4

Resolve

Assistance reaches the affected people.
