📜 Terms & Conditions

Rules and guidelines for using the ResQLink disaster-relief coordination platform.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using ResQLink, you agree to follow these Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the platform.

2. Purpose of ResQLink

ResQLink is designed as a disaster-relief coordination platform that helps connect affected citizens, volunteers, NGOs, relief organizations and emergency response teams.

⚠️ ResQLink should not be considered a replacement for official emergency services. In a life-threatening emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service directly.

3. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for providing accurate and truthful information when using the platform.

  • Provide accurate emergency information.
  • Do not intentionally submit false reports.
  • Do not impersonate another person or organization.
  • Do not misuse emergency communication features.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt or damage the platform.

4. Emergency Requests

Emergency requests should only be submitted when assistance is genuinely required.

False, misleading or malicious emergency reports can interfere with legitimate disaster-response activities and may delay assistance to people who actually need it.

5. Volunteer Responsibilities

Volunteers using ResQLink should follow instructions from authorized response coordinators and should not enter dangerous areas without appropriate authorization, training or protective equipment.

  • Follow coordinator instructions.
  • Prioritize personal safety.
  • Do not perform tasks beyond your training.
  • Report dangerous situations immediately.

6. NGO & Organization Responsibilities

Organizations using the platform are responsible for ensuring that information they provide is accurate and that their personnel follow applicable laws, safety procedures and organizational policies.

7. Location Services

Certain ResQLink features may use location information to display emergency requests, relief centers, volunteers or disaster-affected areas.

Users should only share location information when appropriate for the requested service.

8. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on third-party services such as mapping, hosting, authentication or communication providers.

Availability of those services may affect certain ResQLink features.

9. Platform Availability

We aim to keep ResQLink available and functional, but temporary interruptions may occur because of network failures, maintenance, server problems, disasters or other circumstances.

10. Safety Disclaimer

Disaster environments can be dangerous. Information displayed on the platform may change rapidly and should be verified with appropriate authorities whenever possible.

🚨 Never put yourself in danger to respond to a request shown on the platform. Personal safety should always come first.

11. Prohibited Activities

Users must not use ResQLink for activities that could harm individuals, organizations or disaster-response operations.

  • Creating fraudulent emergency reports.
  • Harassing other users.
  • Attempting unauthorized access.
  • Uploading malicious software.
  • Manipulating emergency information.
  • Using the platform for illegal activities.

12. Account Suspension

Accounts may be restricted or suspended if they are suspected of being used to abuse, disrupt or misuse the platform.

13. Intellectual Property

The ResQLink name, interface, design and original platform content are intended to remain protected according to applicable intellectual-property laws.

14. Limitation of Liability

ResQLink is intended to support coordination and communication. It does not guarantee that assistance will always be available, delivered immediately or successfully completed.

15. Changes to These Terms

These Terms & Conditions may be updated as the platform develops. Updated terms will be published on this page.

16. Contact

If you have questions regarding these Terms & Conditions, please contact the ResQLink support team.
